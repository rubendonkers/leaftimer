Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to install Python or other tools?

No! LeafTimer is bundled into a standalone Windows executable (.exe). You simply download and click to run.

Can I choose Restart or Sleep instead of Shutdown?

Yes. LeafTimer includes a simple toggle between Shutdown, Restart, and Sleep modes.

Is LeafTimer safe and free of telemetry?

100%. There are zero trackers, ads, background telemetry, or internet connections required. It executes purely on your local machine.
